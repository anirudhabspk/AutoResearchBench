Progress toward Recursive Self Improvement depends on agents that can solve hard, open ended ML research problems quickly while producing solutions that generalize beyond the experiments they can see.

Open-ended research rewards persistent and compounding effort. The predominant paradigm of open-ended task evaluation in ML focuses on attempting these problems in a 1-shot to few-shot setting [1], but these are long-horizon problems [3], and evals should focus on treating them as such. A model that is able to compound its efforts more efficiently, and do so for longer, can significantly outperform one that initially appears stronger. This is often experienced in the real-world in ways that are not captured by benchmarks.

Vanilla auto-research evals don’t accurately convey this either, as auto research by design hill-climbs on a single train and validation score, and is blind to test-time performance [2]. It can therefore reward overfit solutions, and long auto-research runs can become enemies of generalization.

To address these gaps, we introduce AutoResearchBench, a benchmark of open ended ML research tasks designed to measure how quickly agents improve over long AutoResearch runs while still generalizing to hidden test data.

Measuring both requires separating the feedback the agent can see from the hidden performance used to rank it. We therefore evaluate each run along two axes:

1

Progress over time.

During the run, the agent optimizes the validation score it can see. We report the Area under the Auto Research Curve (AUARC) to measure how much it improves and how early those improvements arrive.

2

Hidden test generalization.

We rank systems using hidden test AUARC from checkpoints selected using validation alone. We also report final hidden test performance so major breakthroughs that happen late remain visible.

Results

AUARC means Area under the Auto Research Curve. It is the average score across 15 turns, so models score higher when they find better solutions earlier. For hidden test AUARC, we use the hidden test score of the checkpoint selected by validation at each turn.
